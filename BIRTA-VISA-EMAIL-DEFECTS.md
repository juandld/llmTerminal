## Defects, verified against the live API payload

1. **Fake hand-typed quote block + duplicated Birta reply.** Body ends with sign-off
   "David" (line 16 of the decoded text), immediately followed by a plain-text
   `On Sun, Aug 30, 2026 at 9:22 AM Birta Bjargardóttir  wrote:` block with `>` markers
   (lines 18–26) — this was typed into the message body by the agent in the original
   drafting session, not generated by Gmail's quoting mechanism. The signature block
   follows *after* this fake quote (lines 29–34), and Gmail's own real quote of the same
   Aug 30 message (collapsible, with full `<birta@crankwheel.com>` header) comes *after
   that* (line 37 on). Net effect: Birta's Aug 30 reply appears twice, with the signature
   stranded between the fake copy and the real one.
2. **"David" sign-off duplicated.** Confirmed one bare `David` line (line 16) sitting
   above the signature's "David Lopez" — a byproduct of defect 1: the sign-off guard
   only checks the last line of the body, and the pasted fake quote pushed the real
   sign-off up and out of reach of the guard.
3. **Phone number truncated to `+1 (934) 223-230`.** Confirmed in the signature attached
   to this send (line 32: `223-230`, missing the final digit). Older signatures further
   down the same thread (from earlier, pre-Jul-9 sends deeper in history) correctly show
   `223-2303` (e.g. line 126) — proving the truncation is specific to the current
   `camoHero/signatures/crankwheel.html` template, not a one-off.
4. **Email link `href` points to `joi@crankwheel.com` while displaying `david@`.**
   Confirmed directly in the raw HTML: the anchor around the visible text
   `david@crankwheel.com` has `href="mailto:joi@crankwheel.com"`.
5. **Address/name line renders in purple.** Confirmed: `rgb(80,0,80)` appears 9 times in
   the decoded body's inline styles, applied to the name/address paragraph.

All five defects are confirmed unchanged from the prior session's findings — this was a
verification pass, not a new diagnosis. `camoHero/signatures/crankwheel.html` (read-only
check) still contains the same `mailto:joi@crankwheel.com` href, the same
`223-230` (unterminated) phone string, and the same `rgb(80,0,80)` purple, so defects
